# Privacy policy

Overpass Maps collects nothing. It has no server, no analytics and no account.

**What leaves your browser.** When you click Run, your query goes to the
Overpass server chosen in Settings. Before sending, the extension fills in
`{{bbox}}` and `{{center}}` with the area of the map you're looking at. That
server sees the query and, like any website, your IP address. Nothing is sent
until you run a query. Each server's own policy applies:

- overpass-api.de, run by FOSSGIS e.V.:
  [FOSSGIS privacy policy](https://www.fossgis.de/datenschutzerkl%C3%A4rung/) (German)
- overpass.private.coffee, run by Private.coffee:
  [Private.coffee privacy notice](https://private.coffee/privacy.html)
- maps.mail.ru, run by VK:
  [Mail privacy policy](https://help.mail.ru/legal/terms/common/privacy/) (Russian)
- A custom server: whatever its operator publishes.

**What stays in your browser.** Your query, the last results and your settings
are kept in the extension's local storage, and removed when you uninstall it.

**Links.** "View on OpenStreetMap" in a feature's card opens openstreetmap.org,
under the [OpenStreetMap Foundation's privacy policy](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy).
